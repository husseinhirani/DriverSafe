# HUD companion app (MIT App Inventor)

Android app that connects to the HUD over BLE. It reads the phone's notifications (with the
[Notification Listener extension](https://puravidaapps.com/notificationlistener.php)) and contacts,
sends them to the Pico W when the HUD asks, and places calls to contacts chosen on the HUD.

## Missing: project file

App Inventor projects are stored in your App Inventor account, not on your computer. To add it, sign
in at [ai2.appinventor.mit.edu](https://ai2.appinventor.mit.edu), open the project, choose
**Projects → Export selected project (.aia) to my computer**, and put the `.aia` file in this folder.
